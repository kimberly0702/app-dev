### My Favorite Series: FROM
FROM is my favorite series right now. It's a *horror mystery* show about a town that traps everyone who goes in. It looks normal in the morning, but when night comes scary creatures come out and everyone has to hide inside.

### About the Show
- **Genre:** Horror, Mystery, Sci-Fi
- **Creator:** John Griffin
- **Network:** MGM+
- **First Aired:** February 2022
- **Seasons:** 4 so far, season 5 will be the last

### What It's About
A group of people get stuck in a strange town and no matter which road they take, they end up back in the same place. At night they have to stay safe from the creatures while they try to find out what's going on and how to leave.

### Characters I Like
- **Boyd Stevens** (Harold Perrineau) is the sheriff and he leads the town
- **Tabitha Matthews** (Catalina Sandino Moreno)
- **Jim Matthews** (Eion Bailey)

### Why I Like It
1. It's very suspenseful and I always want to watch the next episode.
2. Every episode gives you more questions.
3. There are a lot of clues like the **Bottle Trees** and the **Boy in White**, so I keep guessing what they mean.

### My Thoughts
- The creepy feeling of the town
- The characters helping each other survive
- The mystery keeps getting bigger
- For me, the scariest part of the show is the town itself.
