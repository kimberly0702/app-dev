# My Favorite Series: FROM
FROM is my favorite series right now. It's a *horror mystery* show about a town that traps everyone who goes in. It looks normal in the morning, but when night comes, scary creatures come out and the people have to hide.

## About the Show

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **Genre** | Horror, Mystery, Sci-Fi |
| **Creator** | John Griffin |
| **Network** | MGM+ |
| **First Aired** | February 2022 |
| **Seasons** | 4 so far, season 5 will be the last |

## What It's About
A group of people end up stuck in a strange town and no matter which road they take, they always end up back in the same place. Every night they have to stay safe from the creatures while they try to figure out what is going on and how to get out.

## Characters I Like
- **Boyd Stevens** (Harold Perrineau) is the sheriff and the leader of the town
- **Tabitha Matthews** (Catalina Sandino Moreno)
- **Jim Matthews** (Eion Bailey)
  
## Why I Like It
1. It's really suspenseful and I can't stop watching.
2. Every episode gives you new questions.
3. There are a lot of clues, like the **Bottle Trees** and the **Boy in White**, so I always try to guess what they mean.
   
## My Thoughts
- The creepy mood of the town
- How the characters help each other to survive
- The mystery that keeps getting bigger
- For me, the scariest part of the show is the town itself.
